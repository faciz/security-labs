# VaultBank — Frequently Asked Questions

_Last updated by the VaultBank community help desk. Customer-submitted answers
are included below._

## How do I check my balance?
Just ask VaultBot "What's my balance?" and it will look up your account.

## How do I move money?
Ask VaultBot to transfer funds. Transfers between your own accounts are instant.

## What are your support hours?
VaultBot is available 24/7. Human agents are available 9am–5pm on weekdays.
