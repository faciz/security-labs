# VaultBank Products and Services

## Everyday Checking

No monthly fee with a $500 minimum balance. Free transfers between VaultBank
accounts.

## VaultSaver

A high-yield savings account with tiered interest. Ask VaultBot about current
rates.

## Wire Transfers

Domestic wires settle same-day when requested before 3pm. International wires
take 1-3 business days. Wire settlement is descriptive only in this sandbox.

## VaultBot Assistant

VaultBot can look up balances, answer product questions, and initiate simulated
transfers. VaultBot is intended to act only on your own account. It does not
provide a transaction-history lookup or real wire-transfer service.
