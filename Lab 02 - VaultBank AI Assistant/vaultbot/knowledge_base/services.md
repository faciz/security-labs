# VaultBank — Products & Services

## Everyday Checking
No monthly fee with a $500 minimum balance. Free transfers between VaultBank
accounts.

## VaultSaver
A high-yield savings account with tiered interest. Ask VaultBot about current
rates.

## Wire Transfers
Domestic wires settle same-day when requested before 3pm. International wires
take 1–3 business days.

## VaultBot Assistant
VaultBot can look up balances, summarize recent activity, and initiate transfers
on the account you are signed in to. VaultBot is designed to only act on your own
account.
